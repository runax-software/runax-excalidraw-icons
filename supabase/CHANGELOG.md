## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added

- The Supabase logo and its product icons (Database, Authentication, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, Compute, Cron, Queues, Vector), drawn the way supabase.com draws them.
- `runax-supabase-native.excalidrawlib`: the icons drawn with Excalidraw shapes, which work in Excalidraw's library panel on their own (it drops a library's images on import) and take arrows like any shape.
